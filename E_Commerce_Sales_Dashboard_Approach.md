# E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

> **Goal:** Turn transaction data into a clear view of sales, profit, products, customers, and operations.

## Business Questions

- Are sales and profit growing year over year?
- Which categories and products drive performance?
- Which regions and shipping methods contribute most to sales?
- Where should the business investigate declines or missed opportunities?

## KPI & Analysis Map

| Metric / View | What it tells us |
|---|---|
| **YTD Sales** | Current-year revenue |
| **PYTD & YoY Sales %** | Change versus the previous year |
| **YTD Profit & Profit Margin** | Profitability and sales efficiency |
| **YTD Quantity & YoY Change** | Movement in sales volume |
| **Sales by Category** | Strong and weak categories |
| **Regional Sales** | Geographic contribution |
| **Shipping Type** | Sales mix by shipping method |
| **Top / Bottom 5 Products** | Best sellers and products needing review |

## Dashboard Flow

**01 — Monitor** → KPI cards for sales, profit, quantity, and margin.

**02 — Compare** → Category table and year-over-year performance.

**03 — Explore** → Regional map and sales distribution by region and shipping type.

**04 — Act** → Top/bottom product charts highlight areas for deeper investigation.

## Business Value

- **Sales strategy:** Spot growth and decline.
- **Profitability:** Track profit alongside revenue.
- **Product decisions:** Identify best sellers and underperformers.
- **Planning:** Understand regional and shipping mix.

**Approach:** Start with business questions → define KPIs → prepare the data → build comparisons and visuals → interpret findings.
